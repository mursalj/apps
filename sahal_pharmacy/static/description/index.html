Built on Odoo, not beside it

This is the difference that shows up six months in.

Medicines are products. Patients, prescribers and suppliers are contacts. Batches are stock lots. Dispensing creates a real sale order and moves real stock; billing posts real journal entries.

So Inventory, Purchase, Sales, Point of Sale and Accounting all work on your pharmacy data directly — your stock valuation, your tax return and your reorder rules are the ones Odoo already gives you. Nothing here is a parallel set of numbers that has to be reconciled with the real ones.

What it does

Medicine catalogue

Prescription, OTC, supplement, medical device and cosmetic. Generic-to-brand relations, active ingredients, dosage forms and routes, structured strength, ATC and therapeutic class, GTIN, cold-chain range, and flags for antibiotic, high-alert, hazardous, paediatric and veterinary.

Prescriptions & dispensing

Pharmacist verification, validity and refills, partial dispensing, and dispensing that creates a real sale order and moves real stock with the batch captured.

Controlled drugs

A restricted register where every movement is logged automatically, handover is limited to a pharmacist, and disposal is witnessed.

Suppliers

Pharmaceutical licence with expiry and issuing authority, approved-supplier status, approval per product, and purchase orders that warn or block on an unapproved or unlicensed vendor.

Customer returns

The batch, the condition it came back in, and a pharmacist's decision to restock or destroy — kept separate from whether the customer is refunded.

Insurance claims

The copay split, the claim's life with the insurer, and the insurer invoiced only once they have agreed.

Dispensing that follows the rules

A prescription is written, checked, verified by a pharmacist, then dispensed — and each of those steps is enforced, not just recorded.

  • Clinical safety before verification. Allergies are matched on the active ingredient, so a brand nobody typed is still caught, and free-text allergies are matched through ingredient aliases. Interactions are checked across the whole script.
  • High and critical findings must be signed off by a named pharmacist before the prescription can be verified or dispensed.
  • Prescription-only medicines cannot be sold without one. Enforced at the till and on the server, so it is not a checkbox somebody can untick.
  • Partial dispensing and refills — the remaining quantity stays on the script.
  • Substitutions are recorded: what was swapped, why, by whom, and whether it was genuinely the same generic.

Stock that knows what it is holding

Batch and expiry

Every medicine batch-tracked with an expiry date, because "how many boxes" is never the whole question.

FEFO

The earliest-expiring usable batch is offered first, on the dispensing screen and at the till.

Expiry as money

Expired and near-expiry stock reported with the value attached, so you can see what it is about to cost you.

Stock intelligence

Out of stock, low stock, dead stock and expiry loss, with a daily internal digest to pharmacy managers. Nothing here contacts a customer.

Expired stock is refused, not flagged. At the counter and at the till. Where local policy allows an override, it is recorded against the pharmacist who gave it — which is the difference between a control and a warning nobody reads.

Wholesale, when you supply other pharmacies

  • Trading licence and expiry on the customer, with approval to buy medicines at all.
  • Credit control — Odoo credit limits plus a credit hold only a manager can release.
  • Order quantities — minimum, maximum and whole-case multiples, enforced on the order.
  • Licence, credit and quantity checked on confirmation, with a recorded manager override where one is permitted.
  • Licensed wholesale supply is correctly exempt from the retail prescription rule — supplying a licensed pharmacy is not dispensing to a patient.

Who does what

Five roles that build on each other, so people see their own job.

RoleCan do
CashierSell over-the-counter medicines and take payment. Cannot verify prescriptions or handle controlled drugs.
TechnicianEverything above, plus prepare and dispense non-controlled prescriptions.
PharmacistVerify prescriptions, sign off clinical warnings, dispense controlled drugs and sign the register.
ManagerFull access — set up medicines, release credit holds, authorise overrides, run reports.
AuditorRead-only across the register, dispensing history and the override log, for compliance checks.

What you install

One module. No suite, no dependency chain of your own to manage.

Technical nameWhat it is
sahal_pharmacyPharmacy Management — the complete app. Everything described on this page is in this one module.

Requirements

  • Odoo 19.0, Community or Enterprise.
  • Standard apps: Inventory, Purchase, Sales, Accounting/Invoicing, Point of Sale and Barcode. product_expiry is installed automatically — it is what gives lots an expiry date and enables FEFO rotation.
Point of Sale note. The till integration is switched on per point of sale with a single setting. A till that is not marked as a pharmacy loads no pharmacy data and behaves exactly as it did before — which matters if you also run a restaurant, a hotel shop or a hardware counter on the same database.

Tested, and you can check

241 automated checks ship inside the module under tests/ — twelve suites covering the product master, suppliers, wholesale, reporting, till isolation, clinical safety, returns, expiry and FEFO, compliance, insurance, stock intelligence and the dashboard. Every suite rolls its transaction back, so you can run them against your own database:

odoo shell -d yourdb --no-http < tests/ph1_product_master_check.py

The module also ships a full README.md covering install, roles, first-time setup and everyday use.
